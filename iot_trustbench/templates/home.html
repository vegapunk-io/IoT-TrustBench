Safety Design

Key Principle

The LLM does NOT make safety decisions. Deterministic Python rules classify first.

LLM Role

Receives classification + evidence, generates plain-language explanation only.

Why It Matters

Prevents LLM from freely deciding safety-critical outcomes based on text interpretation.

Decision Classes

Normal

All readings within normal ranges from registered devices.

Emergency

Multiple readings strongly indicate danger.

Sensor Fault

One sensor abnormal, others disagree.

Spoofing

Invalid, impossible, or unknown device data.

Offline

Telemetry missing or too old.

Uncertain

Insufficient evidence, human needed.
